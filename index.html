01
Jobbers.io
Commission-free global marketplace · Direct client negotiation · 300K+ daily visits
Visit Jobbers ↗
0% commission 300K+ daily visits Direct payments All skill types 150+ countries
How it works

Jobbers is a direct-connect marketplace — no middleman on your payments. You negotiate rates with clients personally. The platform earns through ads and optional premium features, not a cut of your earnings.

The math: Earning ₹1L/month on Fiverr? You lose ₹20,000 to fees. On Jobbers — you keep every rupee. Use this when pitching clients.
Smart tips to stand out
  • Complete your profile 100% — Jobbers surfaces complete profiles first. Use a specific bio: not "developer with 5 years experience" but what exactly you build and for whom.
  • Use both modes: post your services AND actively browse and propose. Most wait. Proposers win more.
  • Since there are no platform fees, you can charge your real rate without inflating 20% to cover Fiverr's cut — use this openly with clients.
  • Always confirm payment terms in writing. Arrange milestone payments (30/30/30/10) for any project over $500.
Best for Freelancers at any level who want maximum earnings and are comfortable managing payment logistics directly with clients.
02
Codeable

Visit Codeable ↗

The 5-stage application process
Stage 1
Application + video quiz
Stage 2
Coding quiz (timed)
Stage 3
Test project (real task)
Stage 4
Live webcam interview
Stage 5: A 90-day probationary trial on real projects. Only apply for work you can 100% deliver — Codeable removes low-performers fast.
How to get accepted
  • Build a solid GitHub repo before applying — WordPress plugins on the WP plugin repository are a major credibility signal. Reviewers look for this specifically.
  • Specialize in a high-demand niche: headless WordPress (React/Next.js frontend), WooCommerce scaling, or WCAG accessibility. These command the highest rates in 2026.
  • The live interview tests customer service scenarios, not just code. Prepare how you handle scope creep, late deliveries, and difficult clients.
  • Once in: respond within the first hour to project posts. Projects flow 24/7. Fast responders win over slow ones, every time.
Best for Experienced WordPress developers who want premium clients without bidding wars and are willing to pass a rigorous vetting process.
03
Toptal
Elite global network · Top 3% of applicants · Fortune 500 clients · No rate competition
Apply to Toptal ↗
Top 3% accepted No bidding wars Guaranteed payments Dev · Design · Finance · PM 200K+ applicants/year
The 4 screening stages
  • Stage 1 — English + personality call (15 min). Tests communication fluency and confidence, not accent.
  • Stage 2 — Timed offline coding assessment. Practice LeetCode Medium-level problems — that's the actual difficulty.
  • Stage 3 — Live pair-programming with a senior Toptal engineer (1 hr). Narrate your thinking out loud even when stuck.
  • Stage 4 — Real test project (1–3 weeks). Document your API, write tests even if optional, and break requirements into tasks first.
How to actually pass
  • Apply with a referral — Toptal states referred candidates are 5x more likely to pass. Search LinkedIn for Toptal members in your domain and ask politely. Most will help.
  • For the test project: a working messy solution beats a perfect unfinished one. Prioritize completion, then polish.
  • Write for the Toptal Engineering Blog after joining — it builds your profile and gets you matched to better, higher-rate assignments.
  • Keep your availability updated constantly. Job matchers reach out based on availability — silent profiles get skipped.
The big unlock: Once in, there is zero rate competition. Clients don't compare your price to a cheaper alternative — they see if you're the right fit for their project.
Best for Highly experienced professionals (2+ years) in dev, design, finance, or PM who want Fortune 500 clients, guaranteed pay, and zero time wasted on proposals.

How the threads work

Three separate threads post on the first weekday of every month. All completely free, no platform, no algorithm — just you and the client.

Thread 1
Who is Hiring? (companies post)
Thread 2
Who wants to be hired? (you post)
Thread 3 (best for freelance)
Freelancer? Seeking freelancer? — both sides post here
Copy this format:
SEEKING FREELANCE | React + Node Engineer
Location: Remote (IST) | Available: 20 hrs/week
Stack: React, Node.js, PostgreSQL, AWS
Recent work: [one real result]
Portfolio: [link] · Email: [email]
Tips to get noticed
  • HN readers are technical and skeptical. Be precise and factual. No marketing language — state your stack and one real result instead.
  • Post in the "Freelancer? Seeking Freelancer?" thread specifically, not just "Who wants to be hired?". The Freelancer thread has higher client intent.
  • Use hnhiring.com — it indexes all HN job posts with search, back to 2018. Find clients who post repeatedly and contact them proactively between threads.
  • Reply to "Who is Hiring?" posts too. Many CTOs posting there also need freelance help and respond to targeted, relevant comments.
  • Build HN karma through real contributions before posting. A thoughtful reply on a relevant thread gets seen by hundreds of readers — passive client discovery.
Best for Developers, designers, and technical PMs who can write concisely and want direct access to startup founders and CTOs — no bidding, no fees, no algorithm.

Contra
Portfolio-first independent platform · 0% freelancer commission · 1.5M+ independents · Vetted clients
Visit Contra ↗
0% freelancer commission Vetted clients only Expert verification badges USDC + PayPal + bank Pro plan: $29/month
How it actually works

Contra is built around your portfolio — think LinkedIn meets Behance, with built-in contracts and commission-free payments. Clients pay a small transaction fee (not you). The free plan lets you create a profile, send invoices, and receive payments. Pro ($29/month) unlocks applying to jobs, search ranking priority, and advanced analytics.

Reality check: Contra has fewer clients than Upwork. The play is to use it as your portfolio hub + payment tool, and drive clients from Instagram/LinkedIn to your Contra profile — not to rely purely on inbound.
Invoice trick: Clients don't need a Contra account to pay you. You can invoice anyone, anywhere, just with their email — making Contra work for clients you found completely outside the platform.
How to stand out fast
  • Get verified as an Expert in a specific tool: Framer, Webflow, Spline, Rive, or Relume. Expert badges are visible in search and directly tied to higher rates and faster client inbound. This is the highest-leverage action on Contra.
  • Use Contra's AI-powered "Portfolio Magic" to launch fast — then customize heavily with your real case studies, real results, and actual client quotes. Generic output looks generic.
  • Fill your profile to 100% — Contra's algorithm surfaces complete profiles in "Discover" where vetted clients browse. Incomplete = invisible.
  • Share your Contra profile link everywhere: Instagram bio, LinkedIn headline, email signature, Twitter. Drive your own traffic here.
  • Upgrade to Pro before you have active leads, not after — Pro unlocks applying to opportunities and raises your search ranking immediately.
Best for Creatives, marketers, and developers who want a beautiful portfolio + zero-commission payments, and are willing to drive some of their own client traffic from social media.
